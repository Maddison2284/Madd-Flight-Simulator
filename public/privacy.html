Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026 · Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version: we collect only what we need to sign you in, run your free trial, and handle your subscription. Payments go through Stripe and we never see or store your card number. We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

Madd-Flight is operated by Maddison Empire. Contact: support@maddflight.maddisonempire.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details (via Clerk): your email address, your name, and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account's name, email, and profile picture. Clerk also keeps sign-in records such as session times, device and browser information, and IP address for security.
  • Subscription details (via Stripe): your plan, subscription status and renewal or end date, and Stripe customer and subscription IDs. Stripe collects your payment details directly. We do not receive or store your card number.
  • Trial and usage data (stored in Cloudflare KV): how much free-trial flight time you have used, and access codes you redeemed and when.
  • Technical data: standard server logs held by Cloudflare (IP address, browser, pages requested, time) used for security and reliability.
  • Cookies and local storage: Clerk sets cookies to keep you signed in. The app uses your browser's local storage for preferences (chosen aircraft, gauge size, recent airports, sound and control settings) and a signed unlock token. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your flight position and route stay in your browser; to show scenery, the app requests map tiles for the area you fly over from Google or OpenStreetMap.

3. How we use it

To create and secure your account, run the 5-minute trial, check whether you have Pro or a free-access code, process subscriptions and cancellations, answer support requests, prevent abuse, and keep the service working. We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

4. Service providers (processors)

  • Clerk: accounts and sign-in (privacy).
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions (privacy).
  • Cloudflare: hosting, the app server, and KV storage (privacy).
  • Google Maps Platform: Photorealistic 3D map tiles, which Google receives tile requests for (privacy). Google sign-in, if you choose it, is also provided by Google.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and may process it in the United States and other countries.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and subscription data while your account is open. Trial and access-code records are kept while your account exists so trials can't be repeated. Stripe keeps payment records as financial law requires. Server logs are kept for a short time by Cloudflare. After you ask us to delete your account we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except for records we must keep for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

You can update your name and email from the account menu. To get a copy of your data or have your account deleted, email support@maddflight.maddisonempire.com from the email on your account. Please cancel any subscription first. You can clear local storage in your browser settings at any time.

7. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information beyond what's needed to provide the service. To make a request, email us; we'll verify it's you through your account email. You may use an authorised agent.

8. Children

Madd-Flight is not for children under 13, and they may not create an account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, keep secrets server-side, and rely on established providers for sign-in and payments. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password or Google sign-in.

10. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and change the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant we will let you know in the app or by email.
